## Where the questions come from

The problems are the **past papers**: the AC80 and AC81 NS I MCQ recalls (with the peer-support keys), the AC81 NS I spot test, and the AC79 spot II (back, head & neck, plus the nervous-tissue stations). Each question sits right after the concept card it tests and is tagged with its source (`AC80`, `AC81`, `AC80 · AC81`, `AC81 spot` …). Spot-test photos are cropped from the recall PDFs, one per station, and shared by the station's two questions.

- **Original wording and option order** are kept; a few long AC81 case stems are trimmed to the facts the question uses, and Thai stems carry an English gloss.
- **Recall notes**: where a recalled key or stem disagrees with the lecture, or the recall is incomplete, the item says so in a *Recall note* and teaches the principle.
- **Excluded**: the *Imaging of head and neck* items (that lecture is not one of the NS I handouts here) and items recalled without options (AC81 “mimics a β1 agonist”).
- **Interactive flowcharts**: P-C4 (where the NMJ signal fails: MG, LEMS, toxins, drugs), G-C1 (Horner: which neuron), X-C5 (facial nerve lesion level from the signs) and S-C6 (reading an audiogram: type and WHO degree). Click any box for why that step exists, load a case, or (audiogram) type your own thresholds, then press *Next step*. The PDF prints the charts without the controls.

## Anatomy 101: cadaver to lecture (NS I gross anatomy)

A second deck for the NS I gross-anatomy lectures, built to link each lecture structure to the real cadaver. Each section pairs the lecture slides with dissection steps: an atlas plate with numbered labels to name, the matching handout figure beside it, and what to find and compare. Connected nerves, arteries and veins have a connection map (flowchart) next to the slide or cadaver photo. Click a box to trace its branches, or hide the names to quiz yourself. The class's NS I tidbits board (3D-app views with handwritten labels and notes) is cut into tiles that sit under the card they belong to, with two tidbits cards of their own (F-C9 facial muscles one by one, T-C9 the four parasympathetic ganglia) and a *Tidbits board* page at the end of each section in the PDF.

## Spot trainer: the gross-lab checklist on cadaver photos

| | Link |
|---|---|
| **NS I spot trainer** | https://punnawit9285.github.io/ns-peer-tutoring/spot/ |
| **RS I spot trainer** | https://punnawit9285.github.io/rs-peer-tutoring/spot/ |
| **NS I tutor answer sheet** (every item of a lab on one page) | https://punnawit9285.github.io/ns-peer-tutoring/spot/tutor.html#lab=1 |
| **RS I tutor answer sheet** (every item of a lab on one page) | https://punnawit9285.github.io/rs-peer-tutoring/spot/tutor.html#lab=1 |

Every structure on the *112-69 Gross Checklist NS I*: 7 labs and 77 items, split into 96 structures. Each is shown on Rohen cadaver photos with the printed numbers removed and a thin arrow on the structure. 92 structures have 1–4 photos (278 views on 110 photos). Four have notes and lecture slides only: rotatores, zygomatic branches of VII, thyroid isthmus and diaphragma sellae.

- **Drill** (big *Drill Lab N* buttons): every structure of the lab once, in random order: pick the name of the structure at the arrow from four choices (default) or switch to typing. A miss does not count and comes back three cards later; the round ends when every structure has been named, then lists the ones you missed to drill again. No daily limit, no grading buttons. After the answer: how to find it on the cadaver, why it matters, a Thai line, a **connection map** (a flowchart in the Anatomy 101 style with this structure's box lit: its nerve supply, branches or drainage, its layer or neighbours; a second map in a tab when there is one; Expand for full screen), the neighbours on the same photo (hover shows their arrow), the other photos, the lecture slide and the past spot stations that asked it. Quiz and Find it show the same map after each answer, and the tutor sheet shows it with the notes.
- **Quiz** (four choices from the same photo; a miss shows where your choice really is), **Find it** (four lettered arrows), **Spot exam** (past stations against the clock) and **Atlas** (every label on a photo, with a hide-names self-test).
- **QR** button (top bar of the trainer and the tutor answer sheet): both trainers' QR codes full screen, for a shared screen; the home page shows them small.
- The home page mirrors the lab checklist and ticks an item once each of its structures is named correctly; students can untick or tick by hand. Every page carries the note ห้ามส่งต่อนอก MDCU · รูปติดลิขสิทธิ์. Progress stays in the browser.
- On photos whose numbers and lines are printed into the image, the arrow follows each printed leader to its real end: round its bends, across dark cavities where a black line cannot be seen, and to the point of a printed arrowhead. Every printed line is erased, so the yellow arrow is the only line on the photo. As a fallback, under every arrowed question (and on the tutor sheet) a note says: ถ้าลูกศรสีเหลืองชี้ลูกศรสีดำเล็ก หรือขีดสีดำอยู่ ให้ยึดตามสีดำเล็ก (ใช้ลูกศรสีเหลืองใหญ่ชี้เลยมันจะเห็นไม่ชัด).

Source and method: [`spot/src`](spot/src). The atlas PDF is read locally and never uploaded; see [legal.md](legal.md).

## Pages

- **`student.html`** (also `index.html`, `dist/deck.html`): Present and Browse modes, *Core only* filter, cloze blanks on every concept card (Space = next blank, Enter = all, R = hide, click a blank to flip it), click an option to see the answer, click a figure to zoom, L opens the Thai explanation, B toggles Browse. Swipe on phones.
- **`tutor.html`** (also `dist/notes.html`): the same cards with every answer and blank revealed, Thai bullets, a SAY line on each concept card and core question, the 6-video plan on the start card, an "Understand it: the 80%" summary on each section card, and Thai tutor notes beyond the slides.
- **`student.pdf`**: A4 handout. Each concept on one page with its blanks empty and numbered, then the quiz on that concept (no answers). At the back: the answer key with Thai explanations, the answers to every numbered blank, and each concept's Thai key points and explanation.
