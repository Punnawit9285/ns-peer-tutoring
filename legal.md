# Legal notice

## Purpose

MDCU students made these peer-tutoring decks and PDF handouts for MDCU students. They are:

- **Educational only.** They exist to help classmates study anatomy and the other NS I / RS I lectures.
- **Non-profit.** They are free, carry no ads, and are not sold or used to make money in any way.
- **For use inside MDCU only.** They are meant for students of the Faculty of Medicine, Chulalongkorn University (MDCU). Please do not share them outside the faculty.

All rights to the material below stay with its owners. This repository claims no ownership of it.

- **Cadaver photographs:** cropped figures from Rohen, Yokochi & Lütjen-Drecoll, *Anatomy: A Photographic Atlas*, 8th edition (© Schattauer / Wolters Kluwer). Each figure is credited with its atlas page.
